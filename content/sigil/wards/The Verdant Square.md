Previously a garden district, many of the plants have died and are nothing more than brown skeletons. Only razorvine grows there now, strangling out the corpses of the other plants, and slowly encroaching on the other Wards. Doesn’t have its own Factor. Anyone who comes through a door that opens here finds themself struggling - a victim for razorvine and cranium rats.

### The Cathedral
Bar/meeting hall built in an abandoned temple, overrun with razorvine. The vines have grown in and swallowed the structure of the building, creating a hollowed-out space thick with the scent of greenery.
