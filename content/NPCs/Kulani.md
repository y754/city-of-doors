# Appearance
A summer eladrin, standing at roughly 5'5. Her skin is dark brown, rich and warm, her eyes a deep sunset-pink. Her hair is dark purple, box braids pulled back into a thick rope down her back, dressed in gold and copper beads. Rings flash in her ears, her nose, at her fingers; her smile is fast and warm, and shows teeth. Tattoos adorn her forearms, leaves and vines winding up her wrists, swirling down to her knuckles; you can see the shapes of insects, butterflies and moths and bees between the flowers.
