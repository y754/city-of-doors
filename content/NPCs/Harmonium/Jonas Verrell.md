# Appearance
A human man, bulky with black hair and a permanent scowl.
# Backstory
Acting as an *investigator* for the Courts of Sigil, Jonas Verrell was an unpopular man, even amongst his peers; too quick to anger, too keen to resort to violence, he was never a man with a lot of friends. Even so, he made a good career as an investigator, showing a level of guile that you might not expect on meeting him.

During this career, he found himself simultaneously in the Feywild, by request of [[the Harmonium]], and under investigation himself for aggravated assault in Sigil, within the Court of [[Vesper Severnis]]. When Severnis's pet oracle, [[Calliope]], absolved him of the alleged crime by way of his presence in the Feywild, she found herself stripped of her rank, arrested for false prophecy, and thrown to the Arena.

Verrell and Calliope were reunited when, not content with the loose end scowling in the bars of the Hive, Verrell's employers had him arrested and dropped in the Arena. He suffered a rough couple of days, being on the receiving end of the party's jibes, Vel's abuses, and the dislike of the other prisoners until finally, he was put in an arena with the party and [[Ares]].

The party used the opportunity to interrogate him, discovering that he had been in the Feywild at the laboratory of a man known as Morrow, a fiendish scientist experimenting with fae souls. Verrell died at Ares's hands - or rather, at his teeth.
