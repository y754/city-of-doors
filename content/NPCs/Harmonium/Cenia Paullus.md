# Appearance
An Aasimar woman, tall and muscular with long, straight black hair, gold eyes, and pale skin. A deep scar mars one cheek, pulls her already-frowning mouth into a deeper expression of displeasure that's rarely, if ever, mollified.
# Backstory
Paullus was stationed on the Material Plane, in a [[The Harmonium|Harmonium]] outpost under the command of Neria Askell. She served her Commander with ferocious loyalty and near-religious fervour, a state which came to an abrupt end when Askell was assassinated - by [[Calyx]]. Paullus captured the fairy, but was unable to save Askell's life.

She has since returned to Sigil, no longer quite trusted in the field, a humiliating demotion for an ambitious woman; her encounters with the party since have been characterised by rage, a particular loathing of Calyx. There's a grudge there to settle.
