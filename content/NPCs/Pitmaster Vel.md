# Appearance
>[!caption|right center-text]
>![[vel_kit.jpg|300]]
>Vel, as drawn by @butchvamp on Tumblr<3

Fey'ri, standing just over six feet tall; gaunt, everything about her sharp edges. Her cheekbones protrude over hollowed-out cheeks, her eyes sunken and shadowed. Her nails are long enough to curve into clawed tips, and meticulously cleaned; her teeth, when her thin lips peel back to show them, are narrow and sharp, as if they've been filed down to points. Her face is long, her neck too. Her shoulders stoop slightly, weighed down by the thick white fur-and-feather pelt she's never seen without. Wings hidden beneath the fur give her a hunched appearance, but she moves fast and smoothly, and her gaunt appearance belies her surprising strength.

Her skin is pale, with grey undertones; her lips are bloodless, her eyes deep, bright, and black. Her hair is white, shoulder-length, and slicked back, with dark grey roots visible at the temples.
