# Appearance
Winter eladrin, roughly 5'8, with pale, sea-ice green skin. He has a square, friendly face, with a big, broad smile that shows a couple of broken teeth; a small scar tugs at his upper lip, and his sea-grey eyes crinkle when he smiles. His hair is scruffy and white, pushed back absent-mindedly from his face.

His skin is covered, head to toe, in tattoos; across the shell of his ear, the corners of his eyes, across his throat and collarbone and beneath his clothes, you can see fine white markings. Words in abyssal and celestial, creatures, abstract shapes; they cover his hands, his wrists, what little of his forearms you can see, and they glitter like sea-salt under the sun. Once or twice, you could swear you see them move.
# Backstory
Kiat's father is Senator Xima Arterys. Arterys began his career as a puppet, with the Hive Factor position being more or less a joke, but has since capitalised on the cheap real estate and significant opportunity for economic development within the ward. After his ascension to Senate, he took the position of Treasurer. Following Saeryx Auvere's closure of the main prison in Sigil, Arterys has allowed the construction of multiple prison-like developments within the Hive, most significant of which is Vel's arena; none of these are official "prisons", to avoid the wrath of the Mercykillers, but *internment facilities*, *work camps*, *rehabilitation centres*.

Kiat, previously an academic in the [[Lady's Ward#Hall of Concordance|Archives]], was removed from his position there and placed with Vel by his father upon the opening of the Arena. To his memory, Vel approached Xima and was initially turned away; Kiat isn't privy to the arrangement they came to, but he knows it's unusual for his father to cave on a bad deal.
