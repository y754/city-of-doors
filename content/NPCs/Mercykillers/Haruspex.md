# Appearance
Tall, pallid, and thin; what little skin can be seen beyond their robes is sickly, as if it hasn't seen light in years. Their voice is a soft, slow, rasp, weighing every word before they speak. Their features are hard to make out beneath the hood they're never without, though get close enough and you'll often see the hint of smile in the shadows.

# Backstory
Their story is mostly unknown, but rumours abound. Talk to any barfly in Sigil, and they'll tell you...
- the Haruspex was the mastermind behind Auvere's rise to power
- the Haruspex foresaw their own ruin, and had their own hands cut off to prevent it
- the Haruspex cut their hands off to become closer to Tyr
- the Haruspex's hands were cut off by the Mercykillers to keep them from casting
- the Haruspex *is* Nilesia, or Darkwood, driven mad by their time in the Mazes and resurrected by Auvere for power over the Mercykillers
- the Haruspex is a demon, sent to destroy the Mercykillers and leave the city defenceless

Naturally, Mercykiller leadership refuses to address any such rumours.
