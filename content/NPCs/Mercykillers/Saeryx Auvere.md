# Appearance
Her skin is charcoal-black, with veins like cracks that glow orange-red. Her eyes are that same volcanic orange, with slit pupils, and her hair is fiery red, slicked back and straight. Her teeth are sharp and white, on show more often than not.
# Backstory
Saeryx Auvere came to the Mercykillers an established warrior; during her time in the Red Death, she honed her skills, and her thirst for vengeance, to a razor point. Following Nilesia's death and the assimilation of the Mercykillers by the Senate, she was known to be a vocal detractor from the political machinations of her superiors - criticism that rapidly turned to action, as she rose sharply through the ranks, demanding more and more independence from the Senate until finally taking her role as Commander.
