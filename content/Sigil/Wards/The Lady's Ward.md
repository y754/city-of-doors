### Hall of Concordance
This is the Palace where the Lady of Pain rules over all of Sigil: filled with Dabus, bureaucrats, and security from all of the various groups in the city, it is a highly regulated building. The Lady's throne room is inaccessible to the public, but many areas - the Hall of Records, the Doors, the meeting rooms - can be accessed, with permission, by citizens.

### Liberty Hall
Harmonium HQ. Previously referred to as the City Barracks, the Hall is a large, imposing building with a distinctly prison-like aspect. Bars on the small, square windows, spiked fences at the perimeter; nothing here screams _welcome_. It is eminently defensible, however, and contains a permanent Door to the Outer Planar city of Melodia, a Harmonium outpost in Arcadia. Since the Harmonium began to expand, the Hall is emptier than in the past, but retains an established unit of over one hundred members.

### The Octavius Estate
Mercykillers HQ. Previously the home of Senator Octavius, upon her death it passed to the last surviving member of her house, the priest [[Nadezhda Octavius]]. Nadezdha rapidly turned her inheritance - money, estate, but not Senate seat - over to the use of the [[Mercykillers]]. These days, the estate never sleeps, always ringing with the sound of Mercykillers moving in and out.
