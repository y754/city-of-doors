The location of many of the City’s wealthier guild artisans, marketplaces, and businesses. This ward has the highest concentration of doors in the City, as beings come from all across the Planes to trade here.

While security is high, daring criminals can make a killing in this part of town; if you can dodge the [[Mercykillers]], the [[Harmonium]], the [[Mutual Trade Association]]'s hired guards, and the eyes of wary merchants and punters, there are rich pickings to be had.

### The Guildhall
The primary market in Sigil; a grand, ancient building, source of artisan goods, magic supplies, and artifacts. Built in an ancient Forum, the market is never truly at rest, with new stalls constantly popping up, breaking down, selling out and vanishing; it's a sensory overload, sights and sounds and smells from all across the multiverse. There is a constant presence of private guards, and clerks can be found in every corner, checking permits and licenses, managing the flow of money and goods in and out of the City.

Thieves should pick their targets carefully; card-carrying members of the [[Mutual Trade Association]] can expect the full protection of the Guild at their back, complete with vicious retaliation against anyone who gets caught.
