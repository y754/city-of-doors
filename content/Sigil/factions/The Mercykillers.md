Previously the faction responsible for city security, in recent years they've turned into a private security force for the higher levels of government, leaving the lower classes to hire mercenaries or fend for themselves. A recent coup by [[Saeryx Auvere]] has once again altered the direction of the Red Death, leading them back to their original purpose: dispensation of Justice. Previously ruled by Factol Alisohn Nilesia - after she was sent to the Mazes for her attempts to overthrow the Lady of Pain, the Mercykillers were lead by a Council for some time, often with input and influence from the Senators. Commander Auvere's swift rise within the ranks, combined with the sudden bloating of the Mercykillers numbers as the recruitment drive built up, eventually came to a head: Auvere wiped out the Council, systematically executing or exiling the corrupt elements, and rapidly got rid of those who weren't loyal to the mission of Justice.
