The Senate was formed to mediate between the esoteric, opaque rule of the Lady of Pain and the earthly demands of the people of Sigil; formed of seven Senators, each head of their house, the Senate passes constitutional law, managing the high-level concerns of the city. They pass laws, manage the city's treasury, and dictate the creation and management of public services. Senators are also the only ones permitted direct audience with the Council of the Dabus, the closest any mortals can get to the [[Lady of Pain]].

The Senate meets in the [[Guildhall#Hall of Speakers|Hall of Speakers]] for public debates when new laws are to be passed, and private meetings are held in both the Hall of Speakers and the [[Lady's Ward#Hall of Concordance|Hall of Concordance]].

### Treasury
The city's Treasury is run by [[Senator Arterys]]; previously, [[Senator Octavius (Deceased)|Senator Octavius]] was treasurer, but upon her death, the duty was taken over by Arterys. Management of the Treasury tends to be left to long-lived races, to minimise the complication of passing these vital duties back and forth, chasing the lifespans of some mortal races.
