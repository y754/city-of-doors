The **Mutual Trade Association** (shortened to **MTA**) is a business consortium located in Sigil that represents the interests of the city's merchants and guildmasters. The MTA does not interfere with individual businesses, instead preferring to leverage its considerable collective wealth and power towards streamlining trade and moneymaking, such as dealing with supply chain issues or cracking down on crime waves. Although the MTA's interests are limited, it is capable of devising and implementing solutions much quicker and more effectively than either the Senate or the militant groups.
